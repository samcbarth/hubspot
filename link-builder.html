HubSpot link builder

Add your portal ID to get direct links into your own HubSpot account. Handy for documentation, Slack messages, and training guides where "go to Settings, then..." wastes everyone's time.

The 3 to 12 digit number in any HubSpot URL, for example app.hubspot.com/contacts/12345678/... You can also paste a whole HubSpot URL. Everything runs in this tab. Nothing is saved or sent.

No ID yet, so links use HubSpot's "your default portal" format.

Paste a link from your portal to get a version anyone can open in their own portal, or swap in the portal ID above.
