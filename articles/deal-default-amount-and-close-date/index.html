HubSpot deal amount, revenue properties, line items, splits, collaborators and Deal Score

A deal's Amount can come from line items or a rep's typing, and the revenue properties only fill in for one of them. The settings that decide it, checked through the API.

Subscription
Sales Hub (ARR and MRR need Professional)
Permissions
Super Admin or edit property settings for deal setup
Time
9 min · Intermediate
Last verified
November 16, 2026

Short answer

Settings, Objects, Deals, Setup decides how a deal's Amount is calculated from line items: TCV, ACV, ARR, MRR or manual entry. The same page sets a default close date, deal splits and collaborators. The revenue properties (hs_tcv, hs_acv, hs_arr, hs_mrr) only fill in when the deal has line items. Line items imported or changed through the API do not update the amount. Splits share credit, collaborators do not, and the AI Deal Score is a win-probability percentage, not a substitute for judgment.

1. The deal setup page

Open Settings, Objects, Deals. The Setup tab has the default deal amount (1), the Create Deal form (2), the default close date (3) and line item owner sync (4):

Default deal amountAmount equals
Total contract value (TCV)All line items, recurring and one-time
Annual contract value (ACV)Value over 12 months
Annual recurring revenue (ARR)Annual revenue from recurring line items and their term (12 months assumed with no term)
Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)Monthly revenue from recurring line items
Manual entryWhatever reps type. Adding or removing line items does not change it.

TCV, ARR and MRR ignore a value typed into Amount. If reps both type amounts and add line items, decide which wins before changing this.

  • Apply default close date to new Deals: end of a period (such as this month) or a time after creation. It keeps new deals out of the "no close date" bucket, but it is a placeholder.
  • Customize the Create Deal form sets which fields reps fill in.
  • Sync Line item owner to Deal owner updates line item owners when the deal owner changes.

2. Revenue properties

The deal revenue group holds Total contract value and Annual contract value (1), and Annual and Monthly recurring revenue (1):

Through the API, a demo deal with a one-time line item returned amount 12500, hs_tcv and hs_acv 12500.00, and hs_arr and hs_mrr 0.00. Deals with an amount but no line items returned all four as empty. ARR and MRR need Sales Hub Professional or Enterprise. If ACV looks wrong, check the line items' billing frequency and term.

3. Line items

  • Line items imported or updated through the API do not update the deal amount. Recalculate or set the amount in the same job.
  • The Line items card shows up to 200 line items.
  • Custom line items outside the product library need the Create custom line items permission. Turning that off forces reps onto price books, see quotes, products and payments.
  • In reports, line item currency matches the deal amount's currency.

4. Splits and collaborators

In the Setup tab's Collaboration section: Split Deals among users (1) and Allow users to add collaborators (2). Both are off in my demo portal.

  • Splits give each user a percentage of the amount, shown on the record and used in forecast totals per user. Agree split rules with sales leadership first.
  • Collaborators worked on the deal but get no credit. A user can be both.
  • If you customized the deal record layout, add the Collaborators card yourself.

For how splits show in forecasts, see the sales forecast.

5. Deal Score

  • An AI prediction of the probability of winning an open deal, from deal properties and activities, shown as a percentage. In my demo portal 10 deals had scores, between 12% and 22%; the internal name is hs_deal_score.
  • New deals usually get a score within about 36 hours.
  • Closed deals stop updating, reopened deals get no score, and setting probability to 0% or 100% without closing still leaves a score.
  • HubSpot sets no "good score" threshold by stage and says scores should not be the only factor in decisions.

6. What is changing for 2027

  • Revenue Hub. Pricing features that feed line items, such as tiered pricing and price books, sit in Revenue Hub. See quotes, products and payments.
  • AI on deals. Deal Score and Deal progression both read deal activity, so logged calls, meetings and emails make them better. See deal pipelines.

Common questions

Why didn't the amount change when I imported line items?

Imports and API updates to line items do not update the deal amount.

Why are TCV and ARR empty on my deal?

They come from line items. A deal with only a typed amount has none.

Does a collaborator get forecast credit?

No. Use deal splits for credit.

How soon does a new deal get a score?

Usually within about 36 hours.
