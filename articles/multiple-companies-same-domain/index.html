Several companies with the same domain: how HubSpot decides

Domain is HubSpot's company identifier, but not every path enforces it.

Subscription
All tiers
Permissions
Company edit access
Time
4 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: manual creation warns if the domain matches an existing company's primary domain; imports deduplicate on primary and secondary domains unless you choose a custom unique property. Companies created through the API, including sync apps, are not deduplicated by domain. If several companies share a domain, automatic association links contacts to only one of them, and you can't choose which.

2. Real duplicates vs branches
