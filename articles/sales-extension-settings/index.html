HubSpot Sales extension settings: BCC addresses and more

Extension settings are in email integration settings.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: manage some extension settings in your email integration settings. Additional BCC addresses are added when you log an email sent with the extension, but not for one-to-one emails sent from a HubSpot record.
