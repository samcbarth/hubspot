Let buyers choose a quantity on a payment link

Tick Allow customers to edit quantity and set limits.

Subscription
Needs HubSpot payments or Stripe
Permissions
Super Admin or Manage payment links
Time
2 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: in the payment link line item editor, select Allow customers to edit quantity and enter a Min quantity and Max quantity. Links created from CRM records turn off after a purchase by default; links from the index page stay active unless you turn on One-time use.
