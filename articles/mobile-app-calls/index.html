Calling and logging calls from the HubSpot mobile app

Two options: your carrier, or HubSpot Calling.

Subscription
HubSpot Calling needs a Sales or Service seat

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: the mobile app can call through your mobile carrier or HubSpot Calling, which needs a Sales or Service seat for supported countries. On Android, you can turn on logging calls made outside the app. Where two-party consent applies, the app plays an alert before recording.
