HubSpot's calendar view: meetings in one place after you connect a calendar

The Calendar item in the Sales menu shows your meetings inside HubSpot once your calendar is connected.

Subscription

Permissions

Short answer

After you connect your calendar, HubSpot's KB says the dedicated calendar view lets you review past meetings, prepare for upcoming ones, and schedule new meetings in one place. It appears as Calendar in the Sales menu. Without a connected calendar, the page shows a Connect calendar prompt.
