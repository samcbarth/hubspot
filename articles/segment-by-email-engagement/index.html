Segment contacts by how many marketing emails they opened or clicked

These counters make engaged and disengaged segments easy.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: Marketing emails opened counts emails opened, increasing at most once per email; Marketing emails clicked counts emails with link clicks, also once per email. Use them as segment filters, with date properties for recency.

Searching contact properties for marketing emails in my demo portal.
