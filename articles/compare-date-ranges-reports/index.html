Compare this year to last year in a custom report

Use Date part or Compare by.

Subscription
Custom reports: Professional or Enterprise
Permissions
Reports access
Time

Last verified

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: compare multiple time ranges with Date part (for example quarter year over year) or Compare by. If no preset fits, choose Custom in the Filter period dropdown.
