Short answer

HubSpot's KB: export submissions from the forms index or from a specific page. You'll get an email with a download link; the file includes the form fields, conversion date and conversion page. Exporting submissions for a specific landing or website page needs Marketing Hub Professional or Enterprise.

1. The forms list

My demo portal has nine forms; several are labeled Legacy editor.

2. Gated downloads

For a download after submitting, use the form's thank-you message or redirect to the file.

3. Related

File upload fields.
