Add Google Tag Manager to HubSpot pages

A Community favorite: how to get GTM, and the events it tracks, onto HubSpot-hosted pages.

Subscription
Marketing Hub Professional or Enterprise, or Content Hub Starter and up
Permissions
Settings access
Time
4 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

In Settings > Content > Pages > Integrations, tick Integrate with Google Tag Manager. HubSpot's KB says the tag manager then runs on all HubSpot-hosted content: landing pages, website pages, blog and knowledge base. External sites need GTM added separately. To track only some HubSpot domains, add the GTM code manually instead. It needs Marketing Hub Professional or Enterprise, or Content Hub Starter or higher.

1. The setting

Pages settings in my demo portal, Integrations tab. GA4, GTM and AdRoll are listed together.

2. Consent

These integrations only track visitors who opted in if your cookie banner requires opt-in. See Google Consent Mode.

Checking the tracking code.
