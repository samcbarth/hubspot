Email sending probation and suspension: what triggers them and how to recover

Probation is the warning. Ignoring it leads to a suspension.

Subscription
All tiers that send marketing email
Permissions
Marketing email access
Time
5 min · Intermediate
Validated
October 11, 2026, 4:40 PM ET

Short answer

Probation is a warning with a review step. When an account is put on probation, a banner appears at the top of the marketing email tool and you review your contacts. Only contacts who gave explicit consent and have engaged with your marketing emails should stay. If the contact issues are not fixed, HubSpot can suspend marketing email sending. A suspension is appealed from the Health tab. On HubSpot's free tools, a suspension cannot be lifted.

1. What triggers each one

Probation: if your account uses HubSpot's free tools, probation starts when an email deliverability metric drops below a threshold, such as a high hard bounce rate. The threshold depends on the sending volume for your industry. The email health dashboard shows where you stand.

Suspension: HubSpot's deliverability protection can suspend marketing email when it detects excessive hard bounces, unsubscribes, spam reports, blocklisting, or abuse complaints, or fraudulent activity such as spam traps. HubSpot's stated monthly limits are a 5% hard bounce rate, a 0.1% spam report rate, and a 3% unsubscribe rate.

2. The probation banner

The banner appears at the top of the marketing email tool. Its text names the problem, for example "Your hard bounce rate is poor." Click Review contacts in the banner to start.

3. Review contacts to end probation

  1. In HubSpot, click More, then go to Marketing > Email.
  2. In the banner, click Review contacts. In the right panel, click Next through the explicit consent and consent information screens.
  3. In a separate tab, delete marketing contacts who have not consented to receive marketing emails. HubSpot also recommends removing contacts without a valid email address.
  4. Select I've reviewed my marketing contacts, click Next, then Done.

After you remove these contacts, the hard bounce rate should start to improve. Review the email health dashboard regularly.

4. If the account is suspended

Open Marketing > Email and click the Health tab. Under How to Fix, click Start. HubSpot asks whether you have contacts you have not emailed in over 12 months and which contact sources apply. Then you opt out, quarantine, or delete the contacts in that segment, confirm the steps, and submit the appeal. HubSpot's Deliverability Team reviews the appeal and contacts you.

Removing bad contacts alone is not enough

HubSpot suppresses contacts that have hard bounced, unsubscribed, or marked an email as spam. Removing those contacts does not restore sending. The appeal is the step that does.

5. Clean up before it happens

  • Keep only contacts who gave explicit consent to marketing email from your business.
  • Suppress contacts who have not engaged for a long time. See unengaged contacts.
  • Exclude hard bounces. See bounce types.
