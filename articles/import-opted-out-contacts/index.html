Import an opt-out list so old unsubscribes stay unsubscribed

Bring your old tool's unsubscribe and bounce list over before your first send.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB recommends importing contacts who hard bounced or unsubscribed in a previous tool as opted out, to protect your sending reputation. You confirm the import by typing the number of contacts. It also notes that to keep these contacts out of your billable total, you can delete them and re-import their email addresses as opted out.

1. Start from Data integration

Imports start from Data integration in my demo portal.

2. Why before the first send

Mailing old unsubscribes from a new system is a common cause of spam complaints. See the deliverability checklist.
