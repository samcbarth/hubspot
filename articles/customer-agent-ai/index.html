HubSpot's customer agent: what it answers and when it hands off

It answers from your content and hands harder cases to your team.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: the customer agent answers questions using synced content, performs configured actions such as booking a meeting, and transfers conversations to a human. With brands, you can create one per brand. Reply recommendations don't require deploying it to channels and don't use HubSpot credits.
