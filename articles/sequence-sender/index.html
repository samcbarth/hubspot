Who a sequence email comes from

Manual enrollment: your inbox. Someone else's: use a workflow.

Subscription
Sales Hub or Service Hub Professional or Enterprise
Permissions
Sequences access
Time
3 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: when you enroll manually, you must choose one of your own connected email accounts. To send on behalf of another user, use a workflow. Each contact can be in one sequence at a time, and users have a daily sequence email limit.
