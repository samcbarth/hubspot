Add live chat to your website with HubSpot chatflows

A chatflow is the widget visitors see plus the rules for who answers. It needs a connected inbox or help desk first.

Subscription

Permissions
Chatflows access

Short answer

Chatflows live under Service > Chatflows, with tabs for Web Chat, Mobile Chat and Facebook Messenger. A website chatflow needs an inbox or help desk with a connected channel. You set the welcome message, assignment, target URLs and display behavior. If a visitor minimizes the chat, HubSpot keeps it minimized across pages and chatflows for 24 hours through a cookie, which can override your pop-open setting.

1. Create it

Open Chatflows and click Create chatflow.

2. Settings that matter

  • Workspace: pick the inbox or help desk. Only ones with a connected channel appear.
  • Welcome message and assignment: who answers, and what visitors see first.
  • Target: the URLs where the chat appears. Use separate chatflows for different languages.
  • Display: whether the welcome message pops open as a prompt on desktop and mobile.

3. The minimize cookie

When a visitor minimizes the chat, the hs-messages-hide-welcome-message cookie keeps it minimized for 24 hours across pages and chatflows. When testing a pop-open setting, use a private window.
