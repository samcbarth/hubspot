SLAs and working hours in help desk

SLA clocks follow your working hours.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: SLA goals set response and resolution targets for help desk tickets. Using SLAs adds four properties, including Time to First Response SLA Due Date and Status. Time to first response in SLA hours and Time to close in SLA hours are populated for help desk tickets only. Goals based on combinations of ticket properties need Enterprise.
