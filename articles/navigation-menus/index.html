Edit your website's navigation menu

Manage links in Navigation menus; templates choose which menu shows.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: create menus in Settings > Content > Navigation menus or in the global content editor. Menus can have parent and child items. Editing in settings needs Website Settings permission; in the global content editor, Global content and theme settings permission.
