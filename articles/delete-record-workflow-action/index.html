Delete contacts with a workflow, and get them back

Deleted by a workflow, restorable for 90 days.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: Delete contact automatically deletes enrolled contacts, for example inactive contacts in bulk. Contacts can be restored within 90 days, and the restore tool can filter by the workflow that deleted them. Turn off that workflow first so restored contacts aren't deleted again.

1. Restore
