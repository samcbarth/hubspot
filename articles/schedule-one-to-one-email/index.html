Schedule a one-to-one email to send later

Scheduled emails can't be edited.

Subscription
All tiers; best time Sales Hub Professional+
Permissions
Connected inbox
Time
2 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: schedule up to 6 months ahead. Once scheduled, an email can't be edited, only deleted and replaced. Don't use the tab key to pick send options, or tracking isn't added. Best time to send needs Sales Hub Professional or Enterprise.
