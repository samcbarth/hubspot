Short answer

HubSpot's KB: with Chat transcript on, the contact who started the chat gets the full transcript by email after the associated help desk ticket is Closed, three minutes later. If the ticket reopens before then, it sends when it closes again. Turning it on needs Super Admin and Account Access.
