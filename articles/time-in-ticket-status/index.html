Time in ticket status properties are empty

Check the stage calculated properties switch for that pipeline.

Subscription

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: stage calculated properties are turned off by default for new pipelines and stages. Service stage calculated properties need Service Hub Professional or Enterprise, and toggling them needs Edit property settings or Super Admin.
