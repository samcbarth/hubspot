Why your custom font doesn't show in Gmail or Outlook

Apple Mail shows custom fonts. Gmail and Outlook don't.

Subscription
All marketing email tiers
Permissions
Brand or email edit access
Time
3 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: custom email fonts are supported in Apple Mail and partly in Samsung Mail, but not in Gmail, Outlook or most mobile web clients. Those show the fallback, so pick a fallback with a similar x-height and weight. Google Fonts aren't available for custom modules in email templates.
