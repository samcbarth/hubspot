Who gets notified when a form is submitted

Notifications go to the form creator unless you change them.

Subscription
All tiers
Permissions
Edit forms permission

Short answer

In the form editor's Settings > Submission settings, real-time notifications are sent within a few minutes for submissions not flagged as spam. My demo portal's page says they go to the form creator by default; you can also notify the contact owner when a known contact submits, and add users. Spam gets its own daily digest in the Spam monitoring beta.
