How HubSpot's calendar sync works with Google or Outlook

One calendar provider, primary calendar only.

Subscription
All tiers
Permissions
Your own user
Time
3 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: calendar sync only uses the user's primary calendar, and you can connect Google or Outlook, not both. Events created before you connect won't sync. Deleting a meeting in HubSpot doesn't remove the calendar event.

1. The setting

Calendar preferences in my demo portal, not connected.

2. Keep meetings out

To stop some meetings logging, see the never log list.

3. Related

Recurring meetings not logging.
