Filter segments and reports by association label

Labels only pay off if you can filter on them.

Subscription

Permissions

Time
3 min · Intermediate
Last verified

Short answer

Once associations are labeled, HubSpot's KB says you can filter by them in segments (Professional or Enterprise), workflows and custom reports, where labels can be a filter, an axis or a breakdown. Some objects, such as line items, don't support custom labels. Creating labels needs Super Admin.
