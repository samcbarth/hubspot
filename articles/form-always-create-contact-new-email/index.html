When a form submission creates a new contact, and when it updates an old one

A colleague submits from someone else's browser and overwrites their email. This setting controls that.

Subscription
All tiers
Permissions
Edit forms permission

Short answer

In the updated form editor, Settings > General has Automatically create new contacts from unknown email addresses. The legacy editor's equivalent, per HubSpot's KB, is Always create new contact for email address, which creates a new contact for every new email "regardless of any matching usertokens" from the browser cookie. Without it, a new email from a known browser can update the cookie's existing contact.

1. The setting

Form settings in my demo portal: create new contacts and set them as marketing are both on.

2. Shared computers

Events, kiosks and shared office machines are where this setting matters most.

3. Related

Two contacts, one email.
