Turn incoming emails into tickets automatically

Connect the support address to help desk, and every email becomes a ticket.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: for help desk channels, incoming conversations create tickets automatically. Inbox channels don't create tickets by default. Accounts created after April 1, 2024 can't create tickets from inbox channels and use help desk instead.
