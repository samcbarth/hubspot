One product, several prices: tiered pricing in the HubSpot product library

If the product form only offers one unit price, the account does not have what tiered pricing needs.

Subscription
Revenue Hub subscription and seat for tiered pricing
Permissions
Product library create and edit access
Time
4 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

The product form's Pricing method dropdown offers volume-based, graduated and stair-step pricing, each starting with two tiers. HubSpot's KB says tiered pricing needs a Revenue Hub subscription, and a Revenue Hub seat to create tier-priced products or add them to quotes, invoices and other documents. Without that, the form shows a single unit price. For prices that differ by region, HubSpot points to price books, which also need Revenue Hub.

1. The product library

2. What the form shows without Revenue Hub

In my demo portal, which has no Revenue Hub, the create form's Pricing configuration section has Unit price, Unit cost and Margin only. There is no Pricing method dropdown. I closed the form without saving.

3. The pricing methods

  • Volume-based: HubSpot's contracts API docs say all units are billed at the rate of the tier the total quantity falls into.
  • Graduated and stair-step are the other two options. Check the KB article for how each one calculates before choosing.
