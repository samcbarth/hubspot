Office 365 asks for admin approval when connecting to HubSpot

Your Microsoft admin has to approve the HubSpot Sales app.

Subscription
All tiers
Permissions

Time

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: an "Interaction required" error means your user needs access to the HubSpot Sales application in Azure AD. After consent is granted, your admin may need to manually add users to the HubSpot Sales app from Enterprise applications.
