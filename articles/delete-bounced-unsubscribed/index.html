Delete hard-bounced contacts

Global bounces don't show in filters.

Subscription
All tiers
Permissions
Delete contacts
Time
2 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: create an active segment of hard bounces from the email tool, or use the Email hard bounce reason property. Only bounces in the rolling timeframe are included. Global bounces (three or more HubSpot accounts) don't appear in hard bounce filters.
