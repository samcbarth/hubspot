Make calls from the HubSpot browser

Keep the call window open.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: open the call remote from the top bar; the call window must stay open during calls. Paid Professional and Enterprise accounts need an assigned Sales or Service seat. Calling minutes depend on your subscription, and you're alerted near the limit.
