ACV, ARR, MRR and TCV on HubSpot deals

These come from line items. A deal with only an Amount won't fill them.

Subscription
Sales Hub Professional or Enterprise for ARR and MRR
Permissions
Any user with deal access
Time
4 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: Total contract value is the deal value from associated line items, including recurring and one-time charges. Annual contract value is the value over a 12-month period. ARR and MRR need Sales Hub Professional or Enterprise. If ACV looks off, check line item billing frequency and terms.
