Limit a scheduling page to specific dates

Use a custom date range for an event or conference.

Subscription
Sales Hub or Service Hub; team pages need Starter and up
Permissions
Meeting scheduling access
Time
2 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: choose Over a period of rolling time or Custom date range (Sales or Service seats only), for example for an event or conference. A custom range can't be longer than 10 weeks. Times show in the visitor's own time zone.
