Show purchased products on contacts and companies

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: the Product history card shows line items with price, quantity and last purchase date. It pulls from the 100 most recent closed-won deals and orders associated with the record.

1. In HubSpot

The relevant screen in my demo portal.

2. Worth knowing

Deals need line items for this to show anything.
