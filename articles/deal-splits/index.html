Split deal credit between users

When two reps work a deal, splits give each a share of the amount.

Short answer

In Settings > Objects > Deals > Setup, Split Deals among users lets you share credit. Each user's percentage of the amount shows on the record and in forecast views. Users who worked on a deal but need no credit can be added as collaborators instead, which is a separate setting.

1. The setting

Both options are off in my demo portal.

2. Before turning it on

  • Agree split rules with sales leadership first.
  • Forecast totals use split amounts per user.
