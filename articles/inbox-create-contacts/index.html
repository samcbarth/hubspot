Create contacts from inbox conversations

Starting a chat alone doesn't create a contact.

Subscription
All tiers
Permissions
Inbox access
Time
2 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: visitors aren't added as contacts just by starting a chat; they stay tied to an anonymous cookie until you get an email address. Enter the email in the inbox's right panel to match an existing contact or create one. Messenger guests show as unknown visitors.
