Remove a group of contacts from a workflow

A suppression segment is the cleanest bulk exit.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: in contact-based workflows, use suppression segments, goals or settings to unenroll contacts. Contacts added to a suppression segment are unenrolled and won't enroll while in it. If they're removed from the segment, they don't automatically enroll, even if they meet the triggers. Other workflow types use unenrollment triggers.
