Short answer

HubSpot's KB: the Simple editor can insert a link or image, or click HTML to edit the code. You can set a font with the face attribute, but many fonts aren't supported across email clients. Managing multiple signatures needs Sales Hub Professional or Enterprise and a Sales seat.
