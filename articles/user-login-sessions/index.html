See where you're logged in and sign out of devices

Each device or browser gets its own session.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: a new session starts each time you log in from a different device, browser or app. You can see Last Active and log out of individual sessions, but only your own. You can't log out of your current session this way.

1. In HubSpot

The relevant screen in my demo portal.

2. Worth knowing

This view only covers your own account. Each user checks their own sessions.
