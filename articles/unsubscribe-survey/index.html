Ask why people unsubscribe with HubSpot's unsubscribe feedback survey

The survey only appears when someone unsubscribes from everything.

Permissions

Short answer

In email subscription settings, Unsubscribe feedback survey asks contacts why they leave. HubSpot's KB says it appears only when contacts unsubscribe from all marketing emails, not from one subscription type. Reasons show in the email's post-send summary.

1. The setting

It is on in my demo portal, next to Don't send to unengaged contacts.

2. Use the answers

  • Too frequent: review frequency caps.
  • Not relevant: tighten segments.

3. Related

Unengaged contacts.
