Discount codes on payment links

Codes work on payment links only.

Subscription
Needs HubSpot payments or Stripe
Permissions
Super Admin or Manage payment links
Time
2 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: discount codes reduce price by a fixed amount or percentage at payment link checkout. They're only for payment links, not invoices, quotes or subscriptions, and can't be used with automated sales tax. Inactive codes can't be applied.
