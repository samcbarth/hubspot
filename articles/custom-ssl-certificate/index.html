Add a custom SSL certificate

Needs the add-on and a new CSR.

Subscription
Custom SSL add-on
Permissions
Domain settings access
Time
3 min · Advanced
Last verified

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: by default HubSpot provisions a SAN certificate through Google Trust Services. A custom one needs the custom SSL add-on and a new CSR generated in HubSpot; existing certificates can't be reused. Status shows on the Custom SSL tab.
