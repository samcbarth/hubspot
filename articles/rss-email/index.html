RSS emails for blogs hosted outside HubSpot

Each send includes only posts published since the last one.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: for blogs outside HubSpot, an RSS email sends subscribers new posts from the feed. Featured images aren't added automatically; include them in the feed. Each send only includes posts new since the prior send, even if fewer than the maximum you set.
