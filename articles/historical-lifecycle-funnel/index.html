Historical lifecycle funnels: journey vs legacy funnel reports

Journey funnels count stages completed in sequence. Legacy funnels didn't.

Subscription
Custom funnels need certain Professional or Enterprise subscriptions
Permissions
Reports access
Time
4 min · Advanced
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: legacy funnel reports had no requirement for sequential ordering; journey funnel reports count sequential stage completion. Contacts may not appear if their lifecycle events fall outside the report date range, and HubSpot's example notes a five-year data limit for journey funnels.
