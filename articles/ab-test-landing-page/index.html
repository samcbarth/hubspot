A/B test a landing page, including its form

Form length is one of the things HubSpot suggests testing.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: an A/B test serves two versions at the same URL, with half of visitors seeing each. Compare performance, then select a winner, which becomes the only live version. HubSpot lists form fields as something to test, such as asking only for email versus more information. Rerunning a variation publishes it immediately.
