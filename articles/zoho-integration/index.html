Connect Zoho to HubSpot

Zoho CRM needs the Modified By field on synced objects.

Subscription
Check app requirements
Permissions
Super Admin or App Marketplace permissions
Time
2 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: Super Admin or App Marketplace permissions are required. For Zoho CRM, add the Modified By field to the Zoho objects you want to sync. You choose your Zoho domain during connect and finish in a Zoho window.
