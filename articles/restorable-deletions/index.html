Restorable vs permanent contact deletion

Restorable for 90 days.

Subscription
All tiers
Permissions
Delete contacts
Time
2 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: a restorable delete keeps the contact in the recycle bin for 90 days; restoring brings back activities and analytics. Engagement reports like Productivity still count calls, meetings and tasks of deleted contacts. A permanent delete can't be undone.
