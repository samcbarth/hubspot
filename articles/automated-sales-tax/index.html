Automated sales tax on HubSpot payments

Revenue Hub Professional or Enterprise; US or Canada with HubSpot payments.

Subscription
Revenue Hub Professional or Enterprise
Permissions
Super Admin for payment settings
Time
3 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: automated sales tax needs Revenue Hub Professional or Enterprise. With HubSpot payments, your business must be in the US or Canada. Rates use the customer's billing address; with Stripe, set a tax election in Stripe for local taxes. With it on, each line item needs a unique name. Discount codes don't work on links with automated tax.
