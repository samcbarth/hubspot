Change how dates and numbers display for you

Your format setting changes your view, not tokens in emails.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: personal preferences apply only to your user. Switching language adjusts your date and number format automatically, and the Date, time, and number format dropdown sets it by region. Personalization tokens in marketing emails and workflows use the account default instead.
