HubSpot campaign shows lots of influenced contacts but 0 sessions? You need tracking URLs

The assets are attached and the contacts are influenced, so why does the campaign show no traffic? Because nothing is sending traffic through it.

Subscription

Permissions
Campaigns access and Tracking URLs access
Time
10 min · Intermediate
Validated
October 11, 2026, 5:45 PM ET

Short answer

Influenced contacts and sessions are counted differently. A contact is influenced when they interact with an asset attached to the campaign. A session is a visit, and it is counted only when the visit arrives through a tracking URL with that campaign selected as its UTM campaign. If nobody uses a tracking URL for the campaign, sessions stay at zero. Create a tracking URL for each link you share, select the campaign, and use those links in your content.

1. Find the campaign

Open Campaigns. HubSpot's Marketing Studio lists them under Overview. This demo portal has one campaign.

2. Read the analytics

On the Analyze tab, pick a campaign and read the topline metrics. Influenced contacts and sessions are different numbers. A campaign can show influenced contacts and no sessions, which means its assets were engaged with but no visit came through a tracking link.

Example from my portal

In my portal I had this as an example. One campaign showed hundreds of influenced contacts and zero sessions. You may have something like this.

3. Check the campaign's tracking URLs

Open the campaign details and the Tracking URLs tab. In the demo campaign, no tracking URLs exist, so no visit can register a session for it.

4. Create a tracking URL

  1. Click the settings icon, then go to Tracking & Analytics > Tracking URLs.
  2. Click Create tracking URL. Enter the URL of a page that has the HubSpot tracking code installed.
  3. Pick the UTM campaign. HubSpot requires a campaign name, and visits are counted under it in the campaigns tool.
  4. Pick a Source, such as Email marketing, Paid search, Organic social, or Paid social. Use Other campaigns with a custom source for anything else.
  5. Fill in the optional medium, term, and content fields to name the channel, ad, or post.
  6. Click Create, then use Actions > Copy tracking URL to copy it into your content.

Create one tracking URL for each link you will share, such as the email, the post, or the ad. Link to the asset you most want people to read.

Example from my portal

In my portal the campaign's automatically generated UTM had never been used. After I created tracking URLs for the top assets and the first visit came through, sessions went from 0 to 1. You may have something like this.
