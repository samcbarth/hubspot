SSL certificates on HubSpot domains

HubSpot issues and renews standard SSL for you.

Subscription
All CMS tiers; custom certificates need the Custom SSL add-on
Permissions
Domain settings access
Time
3 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: HubSpot provisions a standard SAN SSL certificate through Google Trust Services when you connect a domain. If your site already has SSL, use pre-provisioning during connection. Certificates renew 30 days before expiry, which needs the domain CNAME to still point to HubSpot. Other certificates need the Custom SSL add-on; you can't bring a pre-existing certificate.
