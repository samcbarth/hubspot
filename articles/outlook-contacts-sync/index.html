Short answer

HubSpot's KB: data sync keeps contacts consistent between HubSpot and your personal Outlook contacts folder. Choose one-way to HubSpot, one-way to Outlook, or two-way, with criteria for which contacts are created. The integration has 14 default mappings that can't be edited; you can add new field mappings.
