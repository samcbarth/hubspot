Enroll many contacts in a sequence at once

Bulk enrollment is capped and throttled, so plan follow-ups after events in chunks.

Subscription
Sales Hub or Service Hub Professional or Enterprise
Permissions
Sequences, Communicate and Bulk enroll permissions
Time
3 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: users with Bulk enroll sequences permission can enroll up to 50 contacts at once. Bulk sends go out at a maximum of three emails per minute and respect your daily send limits, so the last emails can go out later than the first. For a larger list, enroll in batches or use workflow enrollment where your tier allows.

1. Sequences

The sequences tool in my demo portal, before any sequences exist.

2. Plan for volume

Sequences are for one-to-one follow up from a rep's inbox. For a large list, a marketing email may fit better.
