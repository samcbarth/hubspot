Require approval before marketing emails go out

Enterprise only; RSS emails approve once.

Subscription
Marketing Hub Enterprise
Permissions
Super Admin to set up; Approval permission to approve
Time
2 min · Advanced
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: approvals need Enterprise (Marketing Hub for email and social). Only assigned approvers can publish pending content. RSS and blog subscription emails need approval only once. Requests also work on mobile.
