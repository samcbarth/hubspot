Email a HubSpot report or dashboard on a schedule: who can receive it and the 2 hour rule

A home builder wanted a weekly report in two managers' inboxes. The schedule is easy. The recipient rules are what catch people.

Subscription
All tiers; one-time dashboard emails to non-users need Professional or Enterprise
Permissions
Reports access; Super Admin or the owner to edit or delete a recurring email
Time
5 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB says recurring report emails and recurring dashboard emails can only be sent to HubSpot users. Frequency is daily, weekly or monthly. One-time sends are looser: a dashboard can go to non-users if their domain is connected as an email sending domain, and that needs Professional or Enterprise plus admin-level reporting permissions. To stop a schedule, delete it at least two hours before it is due, or it still sends.

1. Recurring means HubSpot users

For a home builder client we set up a recurring email so two managers got a report on a regular schedule. Both were HubSpot users, which is the rule: recurring report and dashboard emails only go to HubSpot users. In the dialog the recipient field is labeled Internal recipients.

2. One-time sends are different

  • Reports: one-time emails can go to non-users through the Other recipients field, and their addresses must be on a verified domain.
  • Dashboards: one-time emails can go to non-users when the recipient's domain is connected as an email sending domain. That needs Professional or Enterprise and admin-level Reporting permissions.
  • HubSpot's limit is 100 emails per day to non-HubSpot users. Contacts or users with bounced addresses cannot receive report emails.

3. Changing or stopping a schedule

For a report: Reporting, Reports, hover the report, Actions > Manage recurring shares. For a dashboard: open it, click Share, then Manage recurring emails. You must be a Super Admin or the owner. Delete it at least two hours before its send time. HubSpot captures the report data two hours ahead, and a schedule deleted inside that window still sends.

4. If someone is not getting them

The dialog points to two things: the recipient allowlisting HubSpot's sending addresses, and their share notifications being turned on.
