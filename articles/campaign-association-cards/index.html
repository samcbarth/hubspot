Associate records with campaigns from CRM cards

Influence type for contacts only.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: use the Campaigns CRM card to associate records. For contacts you can add an influence type; not for deals or tickets. Once a contact is marked influenced, it can't be removed from the influence count.
