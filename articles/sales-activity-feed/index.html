Short answer

Sales > Activity Feed lists your sales notifications from the last year. HubSpot's KB says it shows email notifications only for tracked emails, and real-time notifications fire when a lead revisits your site, a tracked email is opened or clicked, a document is viewed, or a meeting is booked. The number of notifications per month depends on your subscription.
