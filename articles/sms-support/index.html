Text-message support with an SMS channel in help desk

Customers text you; you reply from help desk.

Subscription
Needs an SMS add-on
Permissions
Help desk settings access
Time
3 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: connect an SMS channel to help desk to receive and reply to texts. You can't initiate SMS from help desk or the CRM record, and messages are plain text only, so no images or files. HubSpot uses Twilio as the SMS provider for its SMS add-ons.
