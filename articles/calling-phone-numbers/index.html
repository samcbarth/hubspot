HubSpot-provided vs registered phone numbers

Only HubSpot numbers take inbound calls to shared channels.

Subscription
Check your subscription

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: HubSpot-provided numbers are powered by Twilio and are the only ones that receive inbound calls to shared channels like help desk. Registered outbound numbers are existing lines; each rep verifies their own by SMS or call code, and admins can't assign them.
