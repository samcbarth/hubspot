Credit memos, voids and refunds on HubSpot invoices

Paid invoice: refund or credit memo, not void.

Subscription
HubSpot payments needs Starter and up; Stripe works on all tiers

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: you can't void invoices with payments made against them; refund the payment instead. A credit memo is associated with an invoice when you apply it. Invoices tied to a credit memo, online payment or subscription can't be deleted; with a manual payment, delete the payment first.
