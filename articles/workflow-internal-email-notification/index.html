Email your team from a workflow

Internal notifications go to users and teams, not to contacts.

Subscription

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: Send internal email notification emails specific users, teams or owners, works in all workflow types, and supports tokens from the workflow's object. Send internal marketing email (Marketing Hub Professional and Enterprise) sends an automated email to specified addresses or a property holding an email address. There's also Send in-app notification.

1. The actions

Searching the action picker in my demo portal.

2. Not for customers

To email enrolled contacts, use the Send email action with a marketing email.
