Bot opens and clicks in marketing email stats, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection

Suspiciously high opens and instant clicks are usually machines, not people.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB: with bot filtering on, HubSpot excludes suspected bot activity from marketing email performance, including security scanners, privacy filters like Apple's Mail Privacy Protection, and corporate screeners such as Mimecast. Filtered numbers are usually lower than unfiltered ones. It's on by default, and each email's performance page shows whether it was on for that send.
