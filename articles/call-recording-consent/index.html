Call recording in HubSpot is on by default: consent and exemptions

HubSpot records calls unless you turn it off or add exemptions. Getting consent is your team's responsibility.

Subscription
Calling tiers vary
Permissions

Short answer

HubSpot's KB says recording and transcription are on by default, so all inbound and outbound calls are recorded unless you turn recording off or add exceptions, and it is your users' responsibility to get consent. In Settings > Calling > Call Configurations, Call recording has Record inbound calls and Record outbound calls, each with exemptions by country, phone number or user.
