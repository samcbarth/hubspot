Private content with memberships: access groups and their limits

Access groups decide who can register and sign in to see private pages.

Subscription
Content Hub Professional or Enterprise for pages and blogs
Permissions
Segment edit permission
Time
4 min · Intermediate
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

Memberships organize who can see private content into access groups, which also control registration and sign-in. HubSpot's KB says pages and blog posts need Content Hub Professional or Enterprise (knowledge base and customer portal privacy is Service Hub). Professional allows up to two access groups; Enterprise allows up to 100. Legacy CMS Hub Professional does not support memberships.

1. Access groups

My demo portal has no access groups yet. A Memberships CRM card shows membership on contact records.

2. Simpler option

For one page, a password may be enough. See password protecting a page.

3. Related

Registration emails can be resent from the members list.
