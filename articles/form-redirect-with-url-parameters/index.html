Redirect after a form submit, including conditional redirects

A legacy form can send different people to different pages.

Short answer

HubSpot's KB (legacy editor): redirect to a meeting scheduling page, HubSpot page or external URL, or conditionally redirect by field value, for example sending a region to its rep's meeting link. On HubSpot pages, the page editor's form module setting overrides the form's redirect.
