Blog subscription emails: why the subscribe form is missing, and how frequency works

No published subscription email means no subscribe form on the blog.

Subscription
All tiers with a HubSpot blog
Permissions
Blog and marketing email access
Time
4 min · Beginner
Last verified
October 7, 2026

Short answer

Contacts subscribe at a chosen frequency and get an email when posts publish. HubSpot's KB says a subscription form won't appear on a published post unless at least one subscription email for that blog is published. Each contact's frequency is stored in the [Your Blog] Email Subscription property, which you can bulk edit or import. Blog subscription emails count toward your email send limit.

1. Start from the blog

The blog tool in my demo portal. Subscription emails and frequencies are managed in the blog's settings.

2. Subscribers

  • Segment subscribers by filtering on the Email Subscription property value.
  • Change frequency for many contacts with a bulk edit or import.
